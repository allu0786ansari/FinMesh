**Decision needed (P4, before the frozen commit).** The rule as signed ("2 × last epoch's use, floor A16") has no ceiling. The token service cannot tell legitimate queries from an attack, so a bank that spends its whole budget every epoch doubles it every epoch: after k weeks it holds A16 × 2^k, and in a few weeks it can tokenise every candidate, which removes the OPRF's protection (PT2 → 1). The replay measures this ("Ratchet" table). My recommendation: add a ceiling of **4 × A16** (it covers the largest week-18 test peak, 2.3 × A16 on HI-Medium) and report PT2 at the ceiling as the worst case. The replay already reports both, so the choice can be made from its numbers.

1. Among candidates within the H1c margin on every assessable target of both datasets, it picks the largest share of plain S1's gain.
2. Ties go to the smaller ε, then to the ungated version.
