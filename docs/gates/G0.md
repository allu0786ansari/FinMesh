## What went wrong in the week-2 rule

The rule chose the smallest real-bank option projected to reach 100 test schemes. It projected from train and validation together. That overstates the test count:

- Train schemes are almost never cut by a partition end (53 of 1,582 on HI-Medium), but validation and test schemes often are (243 of 551 and 377 of 500). Their legs in the next partition are masked.
- Sparse federations lose the few legs they can see. A64 had 2.1 multi-member schemes per hour in train but 1.3 per hour in validation.

As a result, the rule chose A64 on HI-Medium, with a projection of 135 and an actual test count of 87. The bias is visible in train and validation alone. **The A64 test count (87) was seen before the amendment; this is disclosed here and in the paper.** G0 counts are readiness counts, not detector results; no model has seen any test data.

## Amendments decided at this gate

| ID | Change | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A5 | **Primary federation fixed by design: B8.** All bank IDs are grouped into eight institutions balanced by accounts, so every account belongs to a member. Real-bank options become a **secondary partial-participation analysis** on train and validation. Projections use the validation rate only. | B8 is the proposal's "four to eight" members and the setting the sharing ladder is about. Under A64, two thirds of every scheme is invisible to all members, so sharing levels would barely differ. Fixing the design removes the fragile projection. |
| A6 | **Headline dataset: HI-Medium** (2,756 schemes, 16 dense days). **Second regime: LI-Medium** (91 test schemes, case-study band, wide intervals). **HI-Small and LI-Small: development and CI-scale replication.** | The proposal's G0 fallback ("move to HI-Medium if it fits the memory budget"). It fits: G0 on HI-Medium peaked at 739 MB, ingest at about 0.9 GB. |
| A7 | **Grounding (H3b) is measured with JSON-only decoding**, not schema-constrained decoding. Constrained decoding stays as the production guardrail; its failure rate is reported separately. | With the candidate enum in the schema, both probed models scored 100% grounding by construction, so H3b as written would be trivially true. |

## Consequences for the plan

- Week 3 builds the evaluation harness for HI-Medium scale and B8 membership.
- The proposal's headline estimates move from the Small sets to HI-Medium. Every run log now lists Small-set commands for quick checks and Medium-set commands for results.
- Secondary analysis added for week 9 or later: detection gain versus consortium coverage (A8 → A64 → B8). This is a natural extension of RQ1: what changes when only some banks participate.
