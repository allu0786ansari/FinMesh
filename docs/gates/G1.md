## Decision

**Proceed** to the sharing layer with RQ1 as the headline. The criterion fixed in the preregistration (section 9, before the run) is met on HI-Medium: paired 95% CI excluding 0, estimate at least 0.05. It is also met on LI-Medium.

**Provisional thresholds unchanged** (owner, 26 Oct): H1b 60%, H1d 80% on under half the accounts, H2a 70%. The preregistration is frozen and the repository tagged `g1`.

## What the evidence says, and consequences for the plan

1. **The oracle's advantage is linking, not detection.** Local models already raise an alert on 82–86% of schemes; the oracle adds only 2–8 points there. In linked detection it adds about 50 points, almost all of it schemes linked across members, which S0 cannot do by construction. So the sharing levels will be judged mainly on whether their tokens and evidence let banks **join** alerts across institutions, not on improving each bank's scores. Weeks 6–7 put the matching layer (OPRF tokens) and fusion at the centre; week 7's fusion rule must link alerts across members using only what each level discloses.
2. **A decomposition is added** (informational, preregistered): S0's own alerts linked with the pooled rule. If it comes close to the oracle, the whole gap is linkability, and S1 (tokens only) may already recover much of it. That would make the leakage side of the frontier (H1c) the deciding question. Your next baselines run produces it.
3. **Artefact ablation added** (preregistered secondary analysis, owner decision). Payment-format share and self-transfers are the strongest features in every model and probably AMLworld simulator quirks. S0 and the oracle are refitted without them, and the gap is reported with and without them.
4. **PT4b direction is positive on validation.** Graph features add AUPRC on HI-Medium and LI-Medium, which justifies the extractor's cost; the confirmatory test is on test data in week 18.
5. **Calibration** improves the Brier score after isotonic fitting. The current ECE (10 equal-mass bins) is uninformative at a 0.2% prevalence, so week 7 will report reliability over the alerting range instead, where calibration matters for fusion.
