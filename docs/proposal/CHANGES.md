| A13 | 26 Oct 2026 | 8.3, 11.2 (X1) | Fusion layer fixed before any real-data X1 result. **Candidates:** every validation account-window with probability ≥ 0.01 or an S0 alert asks about its cross-member counterparties (week 6 asked for alerts only). **Primary fusion:** a per-member logistic stacker (standardised, L2, C = 1) on the level's evidence features (own logit; requests, refusals and alert flags received; inbound requests and their buckets; at S2+ the counterparty record buckets; at S4 the mean counterparty embedding), cross-fitted on the two time halves of validation (windows 39–44 and 45–50), so no row is scored by a model fitted on it. | A15 | 29 Oct 2026 | 8.4, 8.5, 11.2 (X2, X3), 4.2 (H1c, H1d) | Fixed before any real S3, PD or attack result. **S3:** the 20 record fields other than the time bucket and alert flag (S1 content, in the clear) each through k-ary randomised response at ε/20; releases memoised per record; at most ε = 8 per account and epoch, then the S1 answer; ε ∈ {0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8}. **PD:** the S1 answer, plus the S2 record when the requester's score bucket ≥ τ, τ ∈ {2, 3, 4} (p ≥ 0.05, 0.2, 0.5). | A16 | 29 Oct 2026 | 8.4, 8.5, 11.2 (X2), PT2 | **Corrections and sizing found on validation, before test.** (1) **S3 answers every request with a record:** a dummy (probability bucket 0, no deviation, counters missing) when none exists, all through the same randomised response, and the record's time field is the end of the requested range. (2) **Token budget per bank and epoch sized to twice the largest legitimate use on validation** (week-8 X1, seed 0): HI-Medium 74,510, LI-Medium 87,580 (`finmesh/schemas/token_budgets.json`); other datasets keep 250,000. (3) **Attacks A4–A6** fixed before results: A4 ranks an account's first-half record among up to 99 first-half decoys from the same responder (rotation simulated at mid-validation; S3 re-released per half), hit@1 against chance; A5 a classifier on what S4 discloses (deviations, magnitude buckets, embedding) separating autoencoder training rows from other training rows; A6 a responder's label prediction from its own score, then with the requests it received. (4) H1b also reported against the stronger pooled relational configuration (sensitivity). | Week-8 audit: S3's leakage did not fall with ε (LI-Medium fan-in, excess TPR 0.077 at ε = 0.5 and 0.083 at ε = 8; AUC 0.89 against 0.72 for S1) because "no record" was answered in the clear, which reveals a score ≥ 0.01; this contradicts S3's stated guarantee. | A17 | 29 Oct 2026 | 9.2, 10 (tools), 11.2 (X4), 4.2 (H2a) | **Neural track fixed before any neural result.** (1) **Tools:** DP-SGD in NumPy (exact per-unit clipping from Gram matrices, verified against explicit per-unit gradients and finite differences), Google's `dp-accounting` RDP accountant, FedAvg in process; PyTorch, Opacus and Flower are not used.
